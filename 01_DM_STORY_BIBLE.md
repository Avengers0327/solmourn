> **PRIVATE — DM EYES ONLY.** Contains the campaign's core mysteries, the Unbound prophecy, future revelations, and proposed endings. Do not send this file to players.
>
> Edition: **Canon working draft v5.1** · Rules basis: **D&D 5e (2014)** · Format: **eight sessions**, beginning at **level 3**.
>
> **Canon discipline:** `LOCKED` = agreed premise; `WORKING` = developed but revisable; `OPEN` = intentionally undecided. Do not quietly promote a proposal to canon.

## 0. At-a-glance campaign promise

In an apparently last city of humanity, the artificial sun is failing. Ordinary people investigate a blackout, learn who controls warmth, uncover the buried identity of their home, brave the world beyond its walls, and ultimately decide who gets to control the light—or whether people should need a single light at all.

**Core theme:** Light is not good; darkness is not evil. Light can sustain and dominate. Darkness can kill and shelter. What is dangerous is enforced imbalance and the refusal to coexist.

**The player-facing opening question:** Why did the Everflame fail for seventeen minutes, and where did forty-three people go?

**The DM's hidden question:** Can a divided humanity learn to work in Concordance again, even without the original catastrophic circumstances?

## 2. The truth: history in DM order

### A. Discovery (~2180, approximate)

Scientists on 22nd-century Earth find that six apparently different types of anomalous phenomena arise from a **single underlying Resonance field**. As *science fantasy*, the theory fills fictional gaps in the attempted unification of gravity, quantum behavior, information, energy, and matter. It does **not** represent a real established unified field theory. A small minority of people (~1 in 1,000) are innately **Attuned** to one discipline; training and an Anchor are necessary to cast reliable Patterns.

### B. The Division (~2190–2230)

States, laboratories, and businesses separate Resonance into six specialized sectors for power, transport, medicine, cognition, material engineering, and information. Optimization, political monopolies, and incompatible industrial standards drive the modes **out of phase**. This is **Dissonance**: harmful lack of harmonic coordination, not evil, shadow, death, or a seventh element.

### C. The Sundering (~2230)

A globally linked experiment attempts synchronization at planetary scale and fails. A persistent Dissonance boundary forms around Earth, preventing ordinary sunlight and warmth from reaching the surface normally. **The star itself continues burning.** The exact fictional physics and whether it impedes every type of solar radiation are open; *do not* claim that conventional real physics supports the mechanism.

At **one exact shared instant** in the disaster, eight groups at eight locations experience overwhelming fear/despair alongside the united imperative to keep others alive. The field is extraordinarily unstable. Their Resonance, including contributions from some **non-Attuned** people, involuntarily synchronizes. Eight great **Resonant Cores** manifest as eight artificial suns. These are *not* conventional fusion stars, and no group knowingly designed or summoned one. Countless desperate people elsewhere do not reproduce stable Cores—only these eight survive the planetary transition. Why precisely eight remains a historical mystery, not divine moral selection.

### D. Stabilization and the dynasties

The Cores persist but are dangerously unstable and **not infinite-energy generators**. Scientists, technicians, and volunteers build mechanical containment, energy inputs, control systems, heat distribution, and diagnostic infrastructure. The surviving original Concordants become beloved emergency stewards and eventually acquire political authority. In many cities their families become hereditary elites. Over centuries some dynasties are overthrown, democratized, or incorporated into corporate/technical structures.

The Solar Imperium rewrites this into a myth of uniquely chosen **Radiance-only** founding heroes. It suppresses the role of non-Attuned survivors, collective synchronization, the other disciplines, and the seven other cities.

### E. The present day (~700 years afterward)

The Everflame's containment infrastructure and physical machinery are simply **old**. It is failing from ordinary deterioration, deferred repairs, resource shortfalls, and brittle engineering. Dissonance may add stress, but **it is not a covert villain's sabotage** and must never replace the aging-system explanation.

## 3. Cosmology of Resonance

**Resonance:** one field, six learnable expressions. A **Pattern** is an established magical technique; an **Anchor** is a personal focus/interface. Large **magitech** devices can allow noncasters to operate or support Resonance effects without personally casting.

| Discipline | Domain | Narrative boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Radiance | Light, heat, cold, electricity, energy transfer | Does not create unlimited energy. |
| Kinesis | Motion, momentum, force, gravity, spatial shifts | Does not automatically rewrite time. |
| Vitality | Bodies, healing, disease, poison, life and death | Does not provide unrestricted resurrection. |
| Veil | Illusion, perception, emotion, memory, mental influence | Does not equal universal truth or mind control. |
| Form | Matter, structure, shape and physical properties | Cannot create arbitrary permanent wealth/matter. |
| Echo | Information, divination, signals, magical traces and connections | No omniscience or perfect historical playback. |

**Attuned:** roughly 1/1,000 births; only one inherent discipline; learned Patterns require practice. **Trained caster:** rarer still. Provisional city estimate: ~12,000 Attuned, ~900 trained (~600 Crown, ~250 Gilded, ~50 unauthorized); reconcile numbers if play needs precision.

**Unattuned:** no inborn affinity; may use devices and join Concordance. A truly extraordinary Unattuned person can **learn** their first Pattern independently. That is the beginning of the **Singular Unbound**, who may eventually learn *all six*, not at level 3 or immediately on awakening. No secret bloodline or dormant birth affinity.

**Emergent Concordance:** involuntary, shared synchronization during an extraordinary crisis, often fear or despair, provided there are compatible sources and a shared imperative. The First Concordance is a unique planetary-scale occurrence. **Controlled Concordance:** a repeatable learned group technique on a vastly smaller scale. **Collective Unbound:** a group eventually attains **Unity** across six disciplines without every member personally learning every spell.

**Important ethical distinction:** Unity is not conformity. Different voices remain different; Concordance cannot be perfected through coercion. Coerced synchronization is unstable and tends toward Dissonance.

## 4. Solmourn map, society, and institutions

| Concentric zone | Radius from center | Approx. residents | What adventurers find |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Everflame Sanctum | 0–0.7 km | 20,000 | Core housing, secured service stacks, ancient controls |
| Imperial Crown | 0.7–2.5 km | 180,000 | Government and hereditary elite; luxurious, surveilled |
| Gilded Ring | 2.5–5 km | 800,000 | Licensed industry, research, costly magic permits |
| Inner Wards | 5–9 km | 2.5 million | Dense homes, transport corridors, magitech seen as infrastructure |
| Foundries | 9–13 km | 3.5 million | Factories, thermal pumps, labor, technical knowledge |
| Frostward | 13–16 km | 5 million | Cold, rationing, border exposure, mutual aid, neglected repairs |

**Magic visibility:** Crown residents see casting but are watched; Gilded licenses are expensive and **geographically restricted**. Inner Wards have magitech, but personal spellcasting is nearly legendary. Foundries/Frostward use machines without being taught that their systems are magical. The **Office of Resonant Security (ORS)** surveils and detains/captures unauthorized casters for investigation; don't turn it into an always-executing death squad.

**State power:** licenses, Anchors, education, rations, access to the Core, propaganda. **State weakness:** aging infrastructure, dependence on skilled workers, suppressed truth, inability to voluntarily coordinate all six modes.

### Local actors and NPCs

- **Mara Venn** — concerned Frostward resident; asks the party to locate her missing engineer brother. Wants him alive, not an ideological revolution.
- **Tomas Venn** — engineer with knowledge of an affected maintenance station. His suspicion that the light 'isn't dying naturally' is **wrong**, despite being sincere. He may still have discovered malfeasance in maintenance *reporting* without sabotage of the Core.
- **Joren Pike** — caretaker/maintenance witness; knows corridors, routines, and what the blackout sounded like.
- **Inspector Seren Vale** — ORS-linked official or investigator; competent, neither automatically ally nor villain; wants public order and the truth in unequal measure.

Build any additional NPC from: **want / fear / leverage / what they know / what they do if ignored / secret visibility**.

## 6. The prophecy — secret until discovered

The **Sixth Canticle** is a disputed surviving prophecy or predictive record. Its exact text is draft, not a promise that fate overrides the players. A current candidate:

> When borrowed suns begin to wane,  
> And severed voices sing in pain,  
> From those untouched, the chord shall rise,  
> Beneath the veil of starless skies.  
> No flame shall rule, no shade shall fall,  
> As sovereign master over all.  
> One heart or many, freely bound,  
> Shall heal the song beneath the ground.  
> And where the six as one are heard,  
> The world shall wake, its light restored.

**Interpretations:**
1. **Singular Unbound:** born Unattuned; learns Resonance, eventually masters six disciplines.
2. **Collective Unbound:** multiple people, including noncasters using Anchors or engineering, form a free, stable six-mode Unity.
3. Both can occur. No PC is predestined, and no single PC should automatically eclipse the others.

**Reveal protocol:** Do not present this in a player guide or tell a player they are 'chosen'. Seed odd observations in sessions 1–5, then reveal a credible source in session 6. The party's interpretation remains theirs.

| 7 | The Last Procession | Public unrest, Core countdown, coalition choices, controlled Concordance |
| 8 | When Darkness Comes | Repair, distribute, reverse boundary, or hybrid; consequences and epilogue |

**Geography:** Manhattan to Mahwah is well outside the 16 km warmth radius. The expedition needs cold survival, waypoints, heat cells, shelters, supplies, and alternatives to a single fixed route. The **Ramapo Ridge Middle School** reference is a fictionalized preserved educational archive, *not* a magical academy. It may hold a pre-Sundering student project that observed non-Attuned perception of Resonance. A separate relay in the Ramapo Mountains holds transregional signals.

**Never railroad:** If the party does not visit the school, move its essential evidence to an alternate archive, witness, portable copy, or relay backup. Multiple routes; same underlying reality. No mandatory successful skill check to continue the campaign.

## 8. Adventure design: clues, secrets, and contingencies

### The five core revelations

| Truth | Three independent clue routes | Backup if missed |
|---|---|---|
| Everflame fails from wear | Technician logs; worn components; honest third-party maintenance tests | Joren or Tomas can explain the wear in an interview |
| Resonance is one field | Six-mode traces; mixed-discipline apparatus; ancient research fragments | A research device visibly responds to combined inputs |
| Solmourn = old New York | Underground subway/borough map; archived skyline; station equipment inscription | Alternate sealed history vault or public art relic |
| Other cities survive | Northbound relay signal; foreign parts; survivor archive | Incoming contact from another city after relay activation |
| Unity/Unbound is possible | Unattuned anomaly; First Concordance record; Sixth Canticle | Vesperdeep research contact contextualizes earlier evidence |

### Escalation clocks (GM tracking, not player inevitability)

Use 0–6 progress clocks; advance when fiction warrants, not because players took too long choosing:
- **Everflame degradation:** starts 1/6, reaches crisis near session 8, but repair efforts can slow it.
- **ORS scrutiny:** starts 0/6, increases for conspicuous unauthorized magic/intrusion; clever plans can reduce it.
- **Public unrest:** starts 1/6, grows with rations and leaks; can become reform, revolt, or negotiation.

**Never use an invisible clock to force one particular ending.** Tell players the *symptoms* and meaningful options.

## 9. The final decision — no 'correct' light

1. **Stabilize/restore the Everflame:** immediate urban survival; requires physical repair, skilled labor, replacement components, energy inputs, political access, and Dissonance mitigation. Decide its governance afterward.
2. **Restore natural sunlight:** analyze/undo the planet-scale boundary. Requires access to pre-Sundering data, cross-city cooperation, and monumental engineering. Risk: destabilizing existing artificial-sun systems. Not solvable with a single spell or ordinary 9th-level Pattern.
3. **Distributed survival:** share geothermal, local grids, multiple devices, and knowledge. Requires supply chains and political cooperation; leaves darkness with a viable human future.
4. **Hybrid:** repair the Everflame temporarily while slowly dismantling the boundary and distributing infrastructure. Potentially the most credible engineering path; do not privilege it morally.

Finale challenge design: **a social decision, an engineering action, and a Concordance opportunity**. Each PC can contribute a different action or expertise. Add costs and tradeoffs rather than punishing unconventional choices.

## 10. Mechanical canon and unresolved rules

**Resonance Point costs:** cantrip 0; levels 1–9 cost **2,3,5,6,7,9,10,11,13** RP. Full-caster pool follows 2014 DMG spell-point table: **4,6,14,17,27,32,38,44,57,64,73,73,83,83,94,94,107,114,123,133** at caster levels 1–20. Spell levels 6–9 retain DMG once-per-long-rest restrictions. No extra spell slots.

**Amplified Overcasting:** any Pattern, including cantrips and 9th-level, even with sufficient RP; extra +2/+4/+6/+8 RP, unavoidable strain **1d6/2d6/4d6/6d6**, CON save DC **12/15/18/21** to avoid one exhaustion. Up to **twice per long rest**. Only one bounded enhancement; no new effects, free energy, bypassing concentration/materials, or free spell-level access.

**Emergency Overcasting:** once per long rest, borrow max 2 missing RP; 1d6 unavoidable force damage each; CON save DC 10+2×borrowed RP or exhaustion. Cannot stack with Amplification.

**Concordance playtest:** Emergent trial under real crisis (three skill-based stages, DCs 13/15/16, risks). Controlled technique after research: at least two participants and two distinct discipline sources, coordinated actions, 2 RP per caster or equivalent cell per noncaster, DC 15 lead check, concentration up to one minute, one group use per long rest. The scalable, dangerous First Concordance cannot be duplicated by these rules. See player guide for spoiler-safe surface details and app spec for data model.

**Classes:** Warden, Shade, Machinist, Resonant, Votary, Strider; *draft, not balance-tested*. The Resonant one-discipline Pattern catalog is **incomplete** and requires class-specific spell vetting. 2014 rules compatibility matters: don't allow a bonus-action spell plus an action leveled spell illegally; apply standard action/concentration limits. Use the original published spell level for imported spells (e.g., *stone shape* is level 4).

## 11. DM-only boundaries and continuity checks

- Do not call the cities **Pyres** or unilaterally lock **Cinders**.
- Write **the Sundering**, not weird capitalization.
- The **First Concordance is simultaneous, involuntary, desperate**, not a planned technical discovery.
- The founding Concordants becoming elites does not mean all modern governments are monarchies.
- The natural sun is not evil and darkness isn't a foe; no 'purge the night' endings.
- The Everflame's **aging** is physical truth. Investigation may uncover corruption in funding/reporting, but not a retroactive villain who secretly switched off the sun.
- Avoid revealing New York/Mahwah relevance, the other cities, or prophecy too early in player-facing text.
- Don't kill off 43 missing people by default. Their locations, causes of disappearance, and fates remain scenario design decisions, allowing survival/rescue.
- Avoid claiming this entire setting is historically or scientifically realistic: it is deliberately science fantasy, with an internally consistent energy budget.
