# Solmourn: The Dying Light — Character Creation Guide

> **Safe to share with players.** Rules are D&D 5e (2014) with Solmourn homebrew. The six classes and Resonance rules are a **playtest**: the DM may adjust them between sessions, openly.

## The short version

You live in Solmourn, a megacity of 54 million people warmed by the Everflame, an artificial sun. You may be **any official D&D species**. You start at **level 3**. The first session opens in **Frostward**, the crowded outer ring, when the heat fails.

You do not need to know any secret history to make a character. Finding things out is the game.

## Step by step

1. **Species.** Any official D&D species, 2014-era rules. Tell the DM the book and version. Use that one source's rules: never mix two versions or stack two species. For mixed heritage, pick one species' rules and describe the rest.

2. **Ability scores.** Standard array **15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8**, or point buy with **27 points** (8 costs 0, 9 costs 1, 10 costs 2, 11 costs 3, 12 costs 4, 13 costs 5, 14 costs 7, 15 costs 9; scores 8-15 before bonuses).

3. **Class.** One of the six below.

4. **Background and ring.** Two skills from your background. Your ring of birth and where you live now can differ.

5. **Class skills.** Choose from your class list; you cannot pick a skill your background already gave you.

6. **Attunement.** Attuned to one Resonance discipline, or Unattuned. About one person in a thousand is Attuned. Only the **Resonant** class casts; being Attuned without training gives no Patterns.

7. **Numbers.** Proficiency bonus is **+2** at level 3. Ability modifier = (score − 10) ÷ 2, rounded down. Hit points: full hit die at level 1, then half the die + 1 for each further level, plus your Constitution modifier per level.

8. **Equipment.** Your class loadout plus: Cold-weather clothing, 3 days of rations, basic water supply, a district ID or reasonable substitute, and 20 credits.

9. **Who you are.** One goal, one person you care about, one secret (tell only the DM), **one reason you are in Frostward when the lights go out**, and **one existing connection to another PC**.

### Example

Rin is a dwarf Machinist 3 from the Foundries, once disciplined for bypassing an energy meter. She wants to stop heat cuts in the outer ring, has a cousin in the Inner Wards, and knows the party's Warden from a factory accident. She is in Frostward delivering spare parts.

## Species and the Divergence

Many peoples live in Solmourn. All of them descend from Earth's humanity, changed over centuries by biotechnology, Resonance-altered heredity and synthetic engineering. This long history is called **the Divergence**. Your species keeps its published game traits; only the story behind them is told the Solmourn way. Fantasy gods, other planes and fantasy kingdoms are not part of this setting. Species never decides class, district, personality or loyalty.

- **Flying, swimming and climbing species:** use your printed speeds. Solmourn has narrow corridors, sealed tunnels and tall open shafts.

- **Constructed species:** you are a person, not property.

- **Shapechangers:** your printed powers work, but do not automatically defeat every security system.

- **Species with innate spells:** those are limited species features with their printed uses. They do not make you a Resonant and never use or grant Resonance Points.

**In the app:** 10 species have their rules built in (from the SRD). 78 more official species can be chosen and entered from your own book, with the DM checking them. 2024 revisions are not used: the campaign is on 2014 rules.

**Built in:** Human, Variant Human, Dragonborn, Dwarf (Hill), Elf (High), Gnome (Rock), Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Halfling (Lightfoot), Tiefling.

**From your book (bring it):** Dwarf (Mountain), Elf (Wood), Elf (Drow), Halfling (Stout), Gnome (Forest), Aasimar, Firbolg, Goliath, Kenku, Lizardfolk, Tabaxi, Triton, Bugbear, Goblin, Hobgoblin, Kobold, Orc, Yuan-ti Pureblood, Yuan-ti, Aarakocra, Genasi (Air / Earth / Fire / Water), Duergar, Deep Gnome, Eladrin, Sea Elf, Shadar-kai, Githyanki, Githzerai, Changeling, Kalashtar, Shifter, Warforged, Centaur, Loxodon, Minotaur, Simic Hybrid, Vedalken, Leonin, Satyr, Tortle, Grung, Locathah, Dhampir (lineage), Hexblood (lineage), Reborn (lineage), Fairy, Harengon, Dragonborn (Chromatic / Metallic / Gem), Astral Elf, Autognome, Giff, Hadozee, Plasmoid, Thri-kreen, Kender, or another official species. The list was compiled from memory; your DM confirms the book.

## Backgrounds by ring

- **Crown Scion** (Crown): History, Insight. Etiquette and access to some privileged society.

- **Gilded Citizen** (Gilded Ring): Persuasion, Investigation. Commercial credentials and licensed contacts.

- **Ward Resident** (Inner Wards): Insight, Perception. Shelter and local neighborhood rumor network.

- **Foundry Worker** (Foundries): Athletics, Investigation. Trade knowledge, union/coworker contacts, workshop access.

- **Frostward Survivor** (Frostward): Survival, Stealth. Mutual aid and improvised shelter contacts.

Choose two tool or language proficiencies with your DM. Your background does not decide your loyalties. Nobody has to support the Imperium, and nobody has to oppose it.

## The six disciplines

- **Radiance:** Light, heat, electricity, cold and energy

- **Kinesis:** Force, movement, gravity and momentum

- **Vitality:** Healing, anatomy, disease, poison and living systems

- **Veil:** Illusions, perception, emotion and memory

- **Form:** Matter, shapes, material properties and structure

- **Echo:** Information, traces, detection and signals

Resonance is licensed in the inner rings and policed everywhere. Casting where the wrong people can see it has consequences.

## Classes

### Warden

*Shield allies, fight, hold positions. Straightforward martial.*

- **Hit die:** d10 · **Main ability:** STR or DEX · **Saves:** STR / CON

- **Hit points at level 3:** 22 + 3 × Constitution modifier

- **Proficiencies:** All armor and shields, simple and martial weapons. Two of Athletics, Acrobatics, Intimidation, Insight, Perception, Survival. Fighter chassis.

- **Class skills:** choose 2 from Athletics, Acrobatics, Intimidation, Insight, Perception, Survival

- **Starting loadout:** Shield, one melee weapon, one ranged option, medium armor or reinforced coat, survival pack.

- **What you do in a scene:** Hold a doorway while others work; protect a rescuer; stand between a crowd and a cordon.

**Quick-play sheet (level 3)**

- **Fighting Style** (level 1, passive): Works as the published 2014 feature of the same name (see your rulebook).

- **Second Wind** (level 1, bonus, 1 use per short rest): Works as the published 2014 feature of the same name (see your rulebook).

- **Action Surge** (level 2, 1 use per short rest): Works as the published 2014 feature of the same name (see your rulebook).

- **Guard Stance** (level 3, bonus): Bonus action: designate an ally within 5 ft. Until your next turn, when an attacker you can see and within 5 ft attacks that ally, use your reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack.

**Later levels:** 5 Extra Attack · 7 Intercept · 9 Indomitable · 10 Defensive Formation · 15 Last Line · 18 Fortress. Ability score improvements at 4, 6, 8, 12, 14, 16, 19.

### Shade

*Sneak, investigate, disable security. Skill-heavy tactical.*

- **Hit die:** d8 · **Main ability:** DEX · **Saves:** DEX / INT

- **Hit points at level 3:** 18 + 3 × Constitution modifier

- **Proficiencies:** Rogue armor, weapons and tools; four Rogue skills. Rogue chassis with published Sneak Attack progression.

- **Class skills:** choose 4 from Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Performance, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, Stealth

- **Starting loadout:** Reinforced coat, two light weapons, thieves' tools, rope, one disguise kit or forgery kit.

- **What you do in a scene:** Get into the sealed place; find the hatch nobody else saw; lift the document; scout ahead unseen.

**Quick-play sheet (level 3)**

- **Expertise** (level 1, passive): Works as the published 2014 feature of the same name (see your rulebook).

- **Sneak Attack** (level 1, passive): Works as the published 2014 feature of the same name (see your rulebook).

- **Cunning Action** (level 2, bonus): Works as the published 2014 feature of the same name (see your rulebook).

- **Ghost Route** (level 3, 1 use per short rest): Once per short or long rest: if you began your turn in cover or obscurity, your Dash movement this turn provokes no opportunity attacks.

**Later levels:** 5 Uncanny Dodge · 7 Evasion · 7 Silent Entry · 9 False Identity · 11 Reliable Talent · 13 Vanishing Act · 14 Blindsense · 15 Slippery Mind · 17 Perfect Infiltration · 18 Elusive · 20 Stroke of Luck. Ability score improvements at 4, 8, 10, 12, 16, 19.

### Machinist

*Build, repair, scan, improvise devices. Tactical support and problem-solving.*

- **Hit die:** d8 · **Main ability:** INT · **Saves:** INT / CON

- **Hit points at level 3:** 18 + 3 × Constitution modifier

- **Proficiencies:** Light and medium armor, shields, simple weapons, hand crossbows. Tinker's tools, thieves' tools, one artisan tool. Two of Investigation, Arcana, Perception, History, Medicine, Sleight of Hand.

- **Class skills:** choose 2 from Investigation, Arcana, Perception, History, Medicine, Sleight of Hand

- **Starting loadout:** Medium armor, toolkit, Field Rig with two prepared devices, simple weapon, spare parts pouch.

- **What you do in a scene:** Read the gauges, restart the pump, shut down the drone, tell everyone what the machine is about to do.

**Quick-play sheet (level 3)**

- **Field Rig** (level 1, passive): Choose two different devices at the end of a long rest (three at level 9, four at 15). Each has PB activations per long rest, tracked separately. Device attack = INT + PB; save DC = 8 + INT + PB. This is technical equipment, not a pool of spell slots.

- **Technical Expertise** (level 1, passive): Double PB for one selected tool.

- **Jury-Rig** (level 2, action, 1 use per short rest): Once per short rest, action and tools: temporarily restore a small mundane device for 10 minutes. No instant repairs of major city machinery.

- **Engineering Specialty** (level 3, passive): Bulwark (+1 AC with shield), Saboteur (Stealth proficiency or Expertise), or Systems Engineer (Investigation/Arcana Expertise).

**Field Rig devices** (prepare two):

- **Repair Injector** — *Action, adjacent.* Restore 1d8 + INT HP to a construct or repair a small mundane object. NOT living creatures. (2d8 at level 6, 3d8 at 17.)

- **Pulse Scanner** — *Action.* Detect nearby active Resonance technology within 30 ft for up to 1 minute. Requires concentration.

- **Deflection Emitter** — *Reaction.* +2 AC against one triggering attack on you or an ally within 10 ft; choose before the hit is resolved. (+3 at level 10.)

- **Shock Projector** — *Replaces ONE attack in your Attack action.* Ranged device attack, 30 ft, 1d8 lightning (2d8 at level 6, 3d8 at 17). Never a free extra action.

- **Stabilizer** — *Action, adjacent.* Target has advantage on its next CON save before the end of your next turn.

**Later levels:** 5 Extra Attack · 6 Improved Devices · 7 Emergency Repair · 9 Third Prepared Device · 10 Advanced Rig · 11 Precision Engineering · 13 Emergency Override · 14 Reinforced Systems · 15 Fourth Prepared Device · 17 Master Engineer · 18 Redundant Systems · 20 Grand Architect. Ability score improvements at 4, 8, 12, 16, 19.

### Resonant

*Cast learned Resonance Patterns. Resource management and magic.*

- **Hit die:** d6 · **Main ability:** INT · **Saves:** INT / WIS

- **Hit points at level 3:** 14 + 3 × Constitution modifier

- **Proficiencies:** Wizard weapon set, no armor by default. Two of Arcana, History, Investigation, Insight, Medicine, Religion (as cultural studies). Must be Attuned to one discipline. Casting ability INT; spell attack INT + PB; save DC 8 + INT + PB.

- **Class skills:** choose 2 from Arcana, History, Investigation, Insight, Medicine, Religion

- **Starting loadout:** Anchor, Patternbook, light crossbow or quarterstaff, research instruments, insulated clothes.

- **What you do in a scene:** Sense what the field is doing, solve a problem with one well-chosen Pattern, and decide when the cost of more power is worth it.

**Quick-play sheet (level 3)**

- **Patterncasting** (level 1, passive): Cast Patterns from your one discipline using Resonance Points (RP), never spell slots. Requires your Anchor.

- **Patternbook** (level 1, passive): Six 1st-level Patterns at level 1, plus two legal new Patterns each Resonant level. Prepare Resonant level + INT modifier (minimum 1). At level 3: 10 Patterns, max Pattern level 2, 14 RP, three cantrips.

- **Resonance Focus** (level 2, 1 use per long rest): Once per long rest, reduce the RP cost of a level 1-3 Pattern by 1 (minimum 1).

- **Pattern Analysis** (level 3, passive): Arcana proficiency or Expertise for matters involving your own discipline.

**Casting at level 3:** 14 Resonance Points, Patterns up to level 2, 10 Patterns in your Patternbook, three cantrips. RP cost by Pattern level 0-9: 0 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 9 / 10 / 11 / 13.

**Amplified Overcasting** (twice per long rest): +1: +2 RP, 1d6 strain, CON DC 12; +2: +4 RP, 2d6 strain, CON DC 15; +3: +6 RP, 4d6 strain, CON DC 18; +4: +8 RP, 6d6 strain, CON DC 21. One bounded enhancement only.

**Emergency Overcasting** (once per long rest): borrow up to 2 missing RP; 1d6 damage each; CON save DC 10 + 2 per RP or one exhaustion. Cannot be combined with Amplification.

**Patterns available to you at level 3, by discipline:** Radiance 40 · Kinesis 44 · Vitality 33 · Veil 41 · Form 24 · Echo 29. Your DM approves your ten.

**Later levels:** 5 Deep Attunement · 6 Discipline Technique · 7 Resonant Perception · 9 Efficient Channeling · 10 Discipline Mastery · 11 Advanced Weaving · 13 Harmonic Awareness · 14 Discipline Apex · 15 Controlled Strain · 17 9th-level Patterns · 18 Pattern Mastery · 20 Signature Patterns. Ability score improvements at 4, 8, 12, 16, 19.

### Votary

*Heal, rally, organize, negotiate. Support and social. Nonmagical field medic.*

- **Hit die:** d8 · **Main ability:** WIS · **Saves:** WIS / CHA

- **Hit points at level 3:** 18 + 3 × Constitution modifier

- **Proficiencies:** Light armor, simple weapons, healer's kit. Three of Medicine, Insight, Persuasion, Investigation, History, Survival, Perception.

- **Class skills:** choose 3 from Medicine, Insight, Persuasion, Investigation, History, Survival, Perception

- **Starting loadout:** Reinforced coat, 10-use healer's kit, medical satchel, simple weapon, shelter supplies.

- **What you do in a scene:** Keep the hurt alive, calm a frightened crowd, organize an evacuation, speak for people who are not being heard.

**Quick-play sheet (level 3)**

- **Medical Training** (level 1, passive): Medicine Expertise.

- **Field Treatment** (level 1, action, 2 uses per long rest): Action, adjacent target with at least 1 HP: consume one healer's-kit charge AND one use, heal 1d6 + WIS (2d6 at level 5, 3d6 at 14). Cannot affect constructs. Stabilize a downed creature first.

- **Rally** (level 2, bonus, 2 uses per long rest): Bonus action: one ally within 30 ft who can hear you gains 1d6 + WIS temporary HP (2d6 at level 11). Temp HP does not stack.

- **Calling** (level 3, passive): Lifesaver (a creature you stabilize rises to 1 HP, once per creature per short rest), Orator (Persuasion proficiency/Expertise; Rally range 60 ft), or Caretaker (up to six creatures spending Hit Dice during a rest heal +1d4 each).

**Later levels:** 5 Improved Treatment · 6 Steady Hands · 7 Protective Counsel · 9 Emergency Triage · 10 Calling Improvement · 11 Inspiring Recovery · 13 Refuse Despair · 14 Advanced Medicine · 15 Shelterkeeper · 17 Mass Triage · 18 Unbroken Resolve · 20 Beacon of Humanity. Ability score improvements at 4, 8, 12, 16, 19.

### Strider

*Explore, track, survive, fight. A nonspellcasting scout.*

- **Hit die:** d10 · **Main ability:** DEX or WIS · **Saves:** STR / DEX

- **Hit points at level 3:** 22 + 3 × Constitution modifier

- **Proficiencies:** Light and medium armor, shields, simple and martial weapons. Three of Survival, Nature, Perception, Athletics, Acrobatics, Investigation, Animal Handling, Stealth.

- **Class skills:** choose 3 from Survival, Nature, Perception, Athletics, Acrobatics, Investigation, Animal Handling, Stealth

- **Starting loadout:** Medium armor or reinforced coat, bow or spear, climbing gear, snow goggles, survival pack.

- **What you do in a scene:** Find the route, read the cold, track who went where, strike from range, get the group home.

**Quick-play sheet (level 3)**

- **Coldwise** (level 1, passive): Advantage on Survival in frozen terrain; tolerate environmental cold as if properly dressed. NOT resistance to cold damage.

- **Tracker** (level 1, passive): Expertise in Survival or Perception.

- **Fighting Style** (level 2, passive): Archery, Defense, Dueling or Two-Weapon Fighting.

- **Trailcraft** (level 3, 1 use per short rest): Once per short or long rest, lead up to five others for 1 hour (2 hours at level 14) at normal or slower pace without leaving ordinary tracks.

- **Expedition Specialty** (level 3, passive): Hunter (+1d4 weapon damage once per turn to an enemy you already hit this turn), Pathfinder (group overland speed +10% when possible), or Scavenger (Investigation or thieves' tools Expertise plus mundane-device identification after ten minutes).

**Later levels:** 5 Extra Attack · 6 Surefooted · 7 Ambush Sense · 9 Relentless March · 10 Specialty Improvement · 11 Precision Strike · 13 Whiteout Hunter · 14 Unseen Passage · 15 Survival Instinct · 17 Veteran Explorer · 18 Predator's Awareness · 20 Master of the Wastes. Ability score improvements at 4, 8, 12, 16, 19.

## Playing together

- Make someone who can work with the party even when they disagree.

- Tell the DM your lines (never in the game) and veils (off-screen only). Anyone can ask to pause a scene without saying why.

- You can try anything. The DM will say what your character would plausibly know and whether a roll is needed.

- Keep a short note each session: what we learned, what we want, whom we trust, what we still do not know.
