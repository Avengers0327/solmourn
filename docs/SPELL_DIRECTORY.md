# Resonance Pattern Directory (PLAYTEST)

> Player-safe. The discipline mapping and exclusions are WORKING proposals awaiting DM approval. RP cost by level 0-9: 0 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 9 / 10 / 11 / 13. Unearthed Arcana playtest spells are left out of this player copy.

## What is verified

**319 spells are SRD-verified** (official SRD 5.1 text, cross-checked against a second index). **205 other official spells are UNVERIFIED:** their level, school, casting time, range, duration, component letters and book list come from one third-party index (dnd5e.wikidot.com (third-party, unofficial), fetched 2026-11-13) and have not been checked against any official book. No rules text is stored for them and page numbers are not recorded: see the book listed. 12 Patterns are original Solmourn content (playtest).

A spell printed in several books is one row, counted under each book. Where the two sources disagree the row says so (currently: Revivify, Mass Cure Wounds, Mass Heal).

## Mappings that are judgement calls

- **Encode Thoughts** → Echo. Turns thoughts into a physical record: information, so Echo.
- **Infestation** → Form. Conjures pests: left as Form (conjuration default); the DM may prefer Vitality.
- **Absorb Elements** → Radiance. Takes in energy damage and reuses it: energy transfer is Radiance, though printed as abjuration.
- **Beast Bond** → Echo. A shared awareness with an animal: left as Echo (divination default).
- **Cause Fear** → Veil. Fear is Veil even though it is printed as necromancy.
- **Chaos Bolt** → Radiance. Random energy: Radiance (evocation default).
- **Gift of Alacrity** → Echo. Improves initiative by sharpening anticipation: left as Echo (divination default).
- **Silvery Barbs** → Veil. Forces a reroll and grants advantage: probability and attention, mapped to Veil. Echo is the alternative.
- **Fortune's Favor** → Echo. Luck and probability as information: left as Echo (divination default).
- **Shadow Blade** → Form. A solid weapon formed from shadow: shaped matter, so Form rather than Veil.
- **Skywrite** → Echo. Writes words in the sky: a signal, so Echo.
- **Spray of Cards** → Kinesis. Hurls conjured cards: mapped to Kinesis as a thrown force effect.
- **Spirit Shroud** → Vitality. Draws spirits, which have no setting meaning here. Left as Vitality (necromancy default); the DM may reflavor.
- **Galder's Speedy Courier** → Echo. Delivers a message or item through a courier: mapped to Echo as a carried signal.
- **Spirit of Death** → Vitality. Name suggests spirits of the dead. Left as Vitality (necromancy default); the DM should read it before approving.
- **Temporal Shunt** → Kinesis. Printed as a time-themed reaction. Kinesis does not rewrite time, so it is mapped as a displacement effect only; the DM should read it closely.
- **Wrath of Nature** → Vitality. Animates plants and terrain against enemies: Vitality, not Radiance, despite being evocation.
- **Create Homunculus** → Form. Builds a small servant creature. Kept as Form (synthetic personhood is supported); the DM may prefer to exclude it.
- **Create Magen** → Form. Builds a construct. Kept as Form; the DM decides whether the setting's constructs allow it.
- **Dark Star** → Radiance. Darkness is the absence of light, so it sits with Radiance; Kinesis (the gravity theme) is the alternative.
- **Maddening Darkness** → Veil. It is darkness that drives creatures mad: Veil for the madness, Radiance for the darkness. Mapped to Veil.
- **Reality Break** → Form. Printed as conjuration with an otherworldly theme. Left as Form; the DM should read it before approving.
- **Time Ravage** → Vitality. Ages a target rapidly: Vitality (bodies), not a time effect.
