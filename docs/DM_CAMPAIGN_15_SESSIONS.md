**Anchor from the bible:** Frostward heat failure, missing workers, urgent local rescue (§10.2 #1). **LOCKED opening facts (owner decision 2026-11-13):** the opening location is Frostward Thermal Exchange 17; exactly seven maintenance workers disappear in the blackout: four trapped in a collapsed lower service gallery, two detained by Imperial security, and Ilan Rook, who vanishes deeper inside.

**Session goal:** Bring the party together in a Frostward blackout, let them learn that seven maintenance workers are missing, and give them several ways to find out where each went. Nobody has to save everyone, and nobody has to follow every lead.

**Locations:** A ration-queue plaza under a Vigil banner; a stalled public lift; a clinic; the shelter doors; the street outside Frostward Thermal Exchange 17 ("Exchange 17"), an aging thermal distribution and maintenance facility serving Frostward neighbourhoods (site layout in Places).

**Objectives (other than "defeat everything"):** Protect neighbours; choose a rescue order; learn of the seven (four trapped in the collapsed lower service gallery, two detained by Imperial security, Ilan Rook missing deeper inside); reach Exchange 17. The party may pursue all, some or none of the three leads.

**Encounter / hazard:** 2 **Patrol drones** glitching at the Exchange 17 service door, plus the **Steam breach** hazard. Negotiable, avoidable or fought. The collapsed gallery is a hazard to work through in session 2, not a fight.

**Scaling and alternatives:** 3 PCs: 1 drone. 5 PCs: 3 drones. No-combat option: the drones can be powered down at the junction box (tools DC 13) or outwaited at a cost in heat.

**Clues (IDs in Mysteries):** To Exchange 17: clu-courier-route, clu-dispatch, clu-conduit. **NPC agendas:** Mara Venn organizes aid and wants the seven counted (her Cinder role stays hidden). Tarin Holt, a courier, knows the service ways. An ORS field officer (unnamed) arrives last and wants calm. The workers themselves are not present: the four trapped workers, the two detained workers and Ilan Rook are in Places and NPCs.

**Choices and consequences:** br-1-rescue (what you save first) and br-1-workers (which of the seven the party goes after).

**Clocks:** Unrest +1 if the plaza turns into a crush. ORS +1 only for conspicuous Resonance. Tracker "Missing workers accounted for" (0/7) moves when the party learns a worker's whereabouts, whatever the answer.

**Pacing and exit points:** **One-hour stop:** end after the rescue choice and Mara's list of seven. **Optional second hour:** leads, the service door, the cordon. Cliffhanger: from beneath Exchange 17, someone taps four times on a pipe, pauses, and taps four times again.

### Scenes

**1. Blackout in the ration queue** — Describe a mixed crowd: Frostward holds thirty million people of many species. Ask each player: "Where are you, and whom are you trying to help?" Let every class do one useful thing. The Vigil banner over the plaza shows the nine-rayed disk; describe it and move on.

> *Read aloud:* The daylight flickers. For a moment everyone in the queue falls quiet. Then the light goes out altogether. Somewhere beneath the street a pump gives a shriek like torn metal. In the dark you hear people counting aloud, trying to believe the warmth will come back.

**2. Three problems at once** — A lift full of people stalled between floors; the clinic's heat failing; a crush building at the shelter doors. The party cannot be everywhere. Whatever they leave gets worse, not lost.

**3. Mara's list of seven** — The heat authority says nobody was hurt. Mara has seven names from the Exchange 17 night-shift sign-in board: maintenance workers who never came up when the heat failed. **Canon fixes only one name, Ilan Rook;** invent the other six at the table and record them as notes. She does not know where any of them is. **One-hour stopping point.**

**4. Finding Exchange 17** — Three independent routes to the facility: Tarin the courier, the dispatch log, the frozen conduit trail. Give the lead freely; rolls only buy speed or detail. Separately, the party can learn where each worker went: the roster and pipe-tapping point down; booking ledgers and neighbours' accounts point to the detained two; a locker and a badge log point to Ilan Rook.

**5. The service door** — Glitching patrol drones and venting steam at Exchange 17. Talking, tools, stealth and force all work. Do not put any lettering older than the Imperial era on the walls.

**6. The cordon** — Constables arrive, then an ORS field officer. From below the building: tapping.

> *Read aloud:* The door is sealed from the street. Frost has already climbed halfway up the steel. Then, faint and regular, through the pipework under your feet: four taps. A pause. Four more.
