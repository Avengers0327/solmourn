## Hazard: Steam breach
*Any*

- **Effect:** A 10-ft area. Entering or starting a turn there: CON DC 13 or 2d6 fire. Obscures vision.
- **Dealing with it:** Close the valve (STR DC 13), seal it (Seal Breach, tools DC 15), or vent it elsewhere.
- **Tactics:** Use it as terrain both sides can exploit.
- **Scaling:** Raise to 3d6 and DC 15 from level 6.

## Hazard: Cold exposure
*Any*

- **Effect:** A countdown, not repeated saves. State how many rounds or minutes remain before unprotected people are in danger. On expiry: one level of exhaustion.
- **Dealing with it:** Warm Blood, a heat cell, shelter, or restoring flow stops the clock.
- **Tactics:** Tell the players the number.
- **Scaling:** Shorten the countdown for higher stakes.

## Hazard: Evacuation crush
*Any*

- **Effect:** A packed corridor is difficult terrain. Each round with no one directing it, bystanders are hurt.
- **Dealing with it:** Three successes calm it: Persuasion, Intimidation, Athletics to open a second way, Rally, a visible light.
- **Tactics:** Give every PC a different job.
- **Scaling:** Needs five successes in session 6.

## Hazard: Collapsed service gallery
*Levels 3-4*

- **Effect:** A blocked passage with people behind it. **4 successes** open it (Athletics, tools, Resonance that moves or shapes stone and metal, or inventive bracing). A failure after the third success drops grit on the people below (CON DC 12, 1d6, once). A **cold exposure** countdown runs on the trapped: about 4 rounds of work before real danger.
- **Dealing with it:** Shoring it (tools DC 13) cheapens a failure; calling down tells the party where to dig. Rushing may hurt someone below.
- **Tactics:** State the stakes before the first roll.
- **Scaling:** Add a success for a larger collapse.

## Hazard: Dissonance pocket
*Levels 6+*

- **Effect:** A 20-ft zone of unstable Resonance. Roll d4 at the start of each round: 1 gravity lurches (STR DC 14 or prone), 2 senses drop out (blinded and deafened until next turn), 3 discharge (DEX DC 14 or 3d6 lightning), 4 quiet.
- **Dealing with it:** WORKING hazard: local interference, with no will or intent. Patterns cast inside cost +1 RP.
- **Tactics:** Go around it, time the quiet, or steady it with two different disciplines working together.
- **Scaling:** One pocket per site is plenty.
