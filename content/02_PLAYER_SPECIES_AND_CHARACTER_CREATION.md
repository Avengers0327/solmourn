# SOLMOURN: THE DYING LIGHT
## Player Character Creation — The Divergence Edition
**PLAYER-SAFE • Starting level 3 • D&D 5e (2014 baseline)**

### Welcome to Solmourn
Solmourn is a vast, technologically advanced city surviving under an artificial sun, the Everflame. Outside the heat grid, the world is dangerously cold. The Solar Imperium rules the city; different neighborhoods have very different lives. Your character is one of its residents—or someone with a good reason to be there.

### You can play any official D&D species!
Humans, elves, dwarves, tieflings, dragonborn, orcs, goblins, goliaths, aasimar, genasi, warforged, plasmoids, and **any other officially published playable D&D species** are eligible. We will identify the exact sourcebook and edition for unusual choices. In Solmourn, these peoples descend ultimately from Earth's humanity through centuries of biological engineering, Resonance-altered heredity, or synthetic engineering. This history is called **the Divergence**. Your species does not determine your personality, social class, alignment, or political loyalty.

Your species retains its official published gameplay traits; we adapt the story explanation, not arbitrarily rewrite your abilities. Traditional fantasy gods, other planes, and foreign fantasy nations are **not automatically part of this setting**. You can describe your character in familiar D&D terms without importing that species' entire published world.

### Step-by-step build
1. **Choose a species** from an official D&D 5e source and tell the DM which book/version. Use that source's actual rules; do not mix multiple versions or stack mixed-species bonuses.
2. **Choose one of six Solmourn classes** below. The app/DM will provide the finalized class sheet.
3. **Assign abilities:** standard array **15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8** or **27-point buy**; apply species bonuses according to the approved edition/source.
4. **Start at level 3.** Calculate HP, AC, proficiency bonus (**+2**), skills, saves, equipment, and class features using the finalized class rules.
5. **Choose your home district** and occupation/background; the DM will help you choose compatible proficiencies.
6. **Pick a motivation, a relationship with another PC, and a reason to be in Frostward** when the story opens.
7. **Share your sheet for a quick DM review** so unusual abilities, flight, magic, equipment, and character concepts are handled consistently.

### Six playable classes
| Class | Your job | Best if you enjoy… |
|---|---|---|
| **Warden** | Frontline protector and fighter | Combat, defense, holding the line |
| **Shade** | Stealth, infiltration, investigation | Sneaking, clever plans, precision |
| **Machinist** | Technology, devices, repair | Engineering, gadgets, problem-solving |
| **Resonant** | One-discipline Resonance Pattern caster | Spell-like abilities, creativity, risk |
| **Votary** | Field medic and community support | Healing, teamwork, leadership |
| **Strider** | Scout, tracker, survival expert | Exploration, mobility, hazards |

**Resonance note:** A species' innate magical-looking feature does not automatically make you a Resonant. Only the appropriate class/approved feature grants full Pattern casting. Resonants use Resonance Points rather than ordinary spell slots. The six disciplines are Radiance, Kinesis, Vitality, Veil, Form, and Echo. You do not need to master these rules before choosing a class.

### Choose a district
- **Crown:** elite families, state institutions, privilege.
- **Gilded Ring:** wealth, licensed professions, research, business.
- **Inner Wards:** neighborhoods, markets, hospitals, dense urban life.
- **Foundries:** industry, manufacturing, maintenance, engineering.
- **Frostward:** crowded outer districts, unreliable heat, close-knit communities.

Residents of **any species** can come from **any district**. A Crown goblin and a Frostward human may have far less in common than two neighbors of different species.

### Starter gear
You begin with your class's approved starting equipment, weather protection, basic supplies, and identification or a plausible alternative. The DM will adapt equipment to your anatomy without imposing unfair penalties.

### Backstory questions
- Name, species, class, district, occupation?
- What do you want badly enough to risk something for it?
- How do you feel about the Solar Imperium?
- Who matters to you in Solmourn?
- What's one problem, fear, secret, or obligation following you?
- Why are you in Frostward when the opening crisis begins?
- Which other PC do you know, and how?

**Keep it short:** a paragraph is plenty. Do not invent ancient world history, the cause of the darkness, or secrets of the Everflame as established facts. You can propose mysteries about your own character for the DM to develop with you.

### Special character concepts
- **Flying species:** allowed; use published flight rules. Solmourn has narrow corridors, sealed infrastructure and tall open spaces.
- **Aquatic species:** allowed; discuss access to water and equipment.
- **Constructed species:** allowed; you're a person, not automatically property or a robot servant.
- **Shapechangers:** allowed; your published powers work, but they don't automatically defeat every security system.
- **Species with innate spells:** allowed; those are limited species features, not a free Resonant spellbook.
- **Mixed heritage:** choose one complete official species rules package and describe your other ancestry narratively unless an official mixed-heritage option is selected.
