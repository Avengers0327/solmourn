# SOLMOURN — THE DIVERGENCE
## Canon Amendment 001: Playable Species
**Status:** LOCKED at the framework level by campaign owner (Approach B)  
**Visibility:** DM_ONLY (this full document); selected summaries may be published to players  
**Supersedes:** All prior statements that Solmourn is human-only.  
**Does not supersede:** Any other established story, geography, historical revelation, or campaign structure.

## 1. Core canon
All **officially published playable D&D species** are eligible for player characters, subject to an edition/source identification and an appropriate Solmourn explanation. Species are real, socially established peoples in 700 AS. They are **not** newly arrived from fantasy kingdoms or assumed to be outsiders. Their ultimate origins lie in Earth's humanity and its post-Resonance descendants, including engineered organisms, self-sustaining synthetic persons, and unusual Resonance-altered lineages. These origins are collectively called **the Divergence**. The Divergence is an umbrella history, not a single instantaneous event, spell, secret conspiracy, or universal genetic mechanism.

The initial biological and technological branching began after Resonance was discovered around 2180 CE, with additional adaptation and engineering across the seven centuries following the Sundering. Some lineages may have diverged before the Sundering; others are more recent. Their exact origins and lifespans are not all equally documented. A species' traditional D&D name is an accepted common name; in-world alternate terms are optional, not compulsory.

**Humanity** in ancient historical texts can refer to ancestral humanity or to all human-descended peoples collectively; do not infer that every modern Solmourn resident has a standard human body. No original Ember count changes. The nine original artificial suns were formed during the Sundering; species diversification neither created additional Embers nor explains Concordance.

## 2. Non-negotiable continuity rules
1. **Nine original Embers remain nine:** Solmourn (New York), Vesperdeep (Reykjavík), Kharos (Cairo), Aureth (Paris), Namaris (Mumbai), Sevrin (Seoul), Orrakai (Tokyo), Caldris (São Paulo), Azmara (Lagos). Existing government types and politics remain intact.
2. **Solmourn remains Earth:** no automatic Feywild, Nine Hells, Celestia, elemental planes, pantheons, dragons-as-ancient-gods, or interplanetary empires. If a species' printed lore requires these, translate its *origin narrative*, not its mechanics.
3. **Resonance remains six disciplines:** Radiance, Kinesis, Vitality, Veil, Form, Echo. Innate species abilities can be physiology, technology, or limited Resonance expressions; they **do not** grant Patternbook access, Resonant class levels, independent cross-discipline casting, or control of Concordance.
4. **The lost Concordance stays lost:** no species automatically knows it, remembers its founding technique, or inherits an operational Concordance ritual. Species memories or ancestral archives cannot bypass the mystery.
5. **The Unbound is not species-gated:** no species is inherently chosen, universally Attuned, or the prophesied savior. The campaign's outcome remains player-dependent.
6. **The Imperium is unchanged:** Empress Seraphine I Vael is absolute sovereign; House Vael remains united; ORS, Legions, and Solar Ascendancy remain distinct. The dynasty and martial houses can include different species, but hereditary membership is institutional, not evidence of superior species.
7. **Ringism remains central:** place of residence/origin, economic access, licenses, and Imperial loyalty shape opportunity. Species prejudice can exist locally, but there is no new empire-wide species caste or automatic moral alignment. A wealthy Crown goblin can outrank a Frostward human.
8. **Frostward is densely populated:** thirty million residents in the outer ring; not an empty fantasy frontier. The Wastes remain lethally cold, barren territory beyond the heat grid.
9. **Player-safe secrecy persists:** PCs do not begin knowing Solmourn's former New York identity, the nine-Ember truth, the cause of the Sundering, the founding Concordance, or the Unbound's significance. Their species ancestry cannot reveal these facts by default.
10. **The 15-session branching structure stays intact:** Session 6 Mercy Decree, Sessions 8–9 ancient map, Sessions 10–12 rediscovery, and open finale remain. Species may enrich encounters without rewriting reveals.

## 3. Origin families — tools, not rigid biological taxonomies
- **Heritable bioengineering:** longevity, size, musculature, sensory enhancement, adapted metabolism, horns, scales, feathers, gills and similar traits.
- **Resonance-stabilized heredity:** inherited traits from localized exposure or intentional Vitality/Form alterations; does not mean all members can cast Patterns.
- **Synthetic personhood:** autonomous constructed persons, artificial minds, engineered bodies; treated as people, not default property. Their existence is compatible with Solmourn's far-future technology.
- **Adaptive or hybrid lineages:** composite organisms, variable morphologies, symbiotic forms and engineered ecology.
- **Unusual or undocumented lineages:** an approved official species can have a plausible but partially unknown origin; the DM must not turn uncertainty into a free pass for divine/planar revelations.

## 4. Character mechanics — approved framework
1. **Eligibility:** any species with an official published player-character rules entry in an agreed D&D 5e source. No homebrew or third-party species under this amendment unless separately approved.
2. **Edition:** the campaign baseline is **2014 fifth edition**. Use the character's selected official 2014-era species rules, including later official 5e supplements, unless the DM explicitly adopts a 2024 revision. **Do not stack two versions of the same species**, and do not combine 2014 racial ability bonuses with a 2024 background-based ability-score system. For legacy species with revised presentations, identify the exact book and version on the sheet.
3. **Mechanics preserved:** retain the chosen published traits, size, speed, senses, languages, proficiencies, resistances, limited-use powers, flight, and rest mechanics, subject to normal table balance review. Reflavor **narrative source**, not the power's magnitude or frequency.
4. **Ability scores:** use the selected source's applicable 2014-era ability score rules consistently; standard array 15,14,13,12,10,8 or 27-point buy before modifiers. Optional customized origin rules require explicit DM approval and consistent implementation.
5. **Classes:** Warden, Shade, Machinist, Resonant, Votary, Strider remain the only six campaign classes at initial creation. Species never dictates class or faction.
6. **Resonance casting:** only a character with the appropriate class/feature and permission can use Pattern casting. A printed species trait that grants a spell is a **bounded inherited trait** with its printed uses and spellcasting parameters; it does **not** grant the Resonant's RP pool, extra prepared Patterns, or a second discipline. Where a species trait references spell slots, the DM must approve a specific conversion or retain that feature's printed limited-use mechanics; never silently charge or award RP.
7. **Attunement:** a Resonant must be Attuned to their chosen discipline. Other characters can be Attuned or Unattuned in narrative terms, but innate species traits alone do not grant broad Attunement or trained Pattern casting. Approximate innate Attunement rarity (about 1 in 1,000) remains a **world-level population estimate**, not a random character-creation gate for someone choosing Resonant.
8. **Technology and movement:** flying, climbing, swimming, darkvision, unusual anatomy, and changing shape are supported. Use printed mechanical limits and logical environmental consequences (ceiling clearance, air currents, pressure doors, thermal barriers, sensors), not arbitrary negation of abilities.
9. **Mixed ancestries:** use a published half-species/lineage option where one exists, or select **one** published species' full mechanical package and describe the rest narratively. Do not stack species trait blocks.
10. **Size/gear:** standard rules govern size, carrying, armor, and weapons. Specialized equipment is available where reasonably supported by Solmourn's infrastructure; scarcity is a story circumstance, not a permanent species tax.
11. **No species-based alignment:** no species has compulsory morality, loyalty, or caste. Social prejudice can be portrayed with player consent and a Session Zero boundaries discussion.
12. **Balance:** published species features are not to be nerfed solely because the setting is futuristic. Resolve genuine encounter or campaign balance issues transparently and consistently before play.

## 5. How the Divergence affects everyday Solmourn
Mixed-species neighborhoods are normal. Housing, medicine, transit and industrial workplaces support many anatomies, though neglected Frostward services are less accessible. Vitality clinicians know the limits of different physiologies; Constructed persons have maintenance specialists and legal identities. A winged resident can traverse some open shafts but not sealed tunnels; aquatic residents rely on managed water facilities. The ORS tracks people through permits, devices, records, and intelligence—not perfect race-based detection. The Vigil may use inclusive language about all peoples surviving beneath the Everflame; it remains a state-aligned faith, not a species religion. The Empress can command subjects of any species, and the Cinders recruit across species lines.

**Do not retcon the campaign into a species civil war.** A specific NPC's species is OPEN unless already established. Reassigning an NPC species must not silently change their goals, relationships, or political role. Species should offer character texture and tactical variety, not replace the core story of survival, inequality, power, and light.

## 6. Canon statuses and open questions
**LOCKED:** All official playable species permitted; the Divergence's human-origin science-fantasy framework; six classes; nine Embers; no compulsory fantasy cosmology; species traits do not equal Pattern access; no species caste replacing ringism; existing major reveals protected.

**WORKING:** Specific biological explanations for individual species, popular in-world alternative names, approximate dates of each divergence, frequency and district distribution, synthetic-person legal disputes, accommodations and cultural customs.

**OPEN:** Exact official sourcebook list licensed/available to the table; whether the DM opts into any 2024 species revisions; detailed origin of every exotic lineage; demographic counts; species identities of unnamed NPCs; whether particular rare species appear in every Ember.

**RETIRED:** Human-only PC eligibility; blanket statement that all Solmourn inhabitants are standard humans; fantasy species necessarily require fantasy kingdoms or divine creation.

## 7. Spoiler-safe publication boundary
Safe to reveal before Session 1: many species live in Solmourn; the Divergence is a general historical term; biotechnology and Resonance changed human-descended populations; each species has its own abilities; species do not determine loyalty, status, or morality; the Imperium rules the city; the world outside the heat grid is dangerous.

**DM ONLY:** ancient Earth location of Solmourn; other Embers and their current status; nine original Concordances; true mechanism of artificial suns; specific Unbound interpretation; any secret lineage experiment tied to an unrevealed plot; hidden NPC allegiance; later map and relay discoveries.
